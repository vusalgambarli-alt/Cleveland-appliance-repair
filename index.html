FAQ

Common Questions

If your question isn't here, give us a call. We're happy to help.

How much does an appliance repair cost? ▼
Most repairs run between $150 and $450 including parts and labor. We give you an exact flat-rate quote over the phone or at the start of the visit — before any work begins. No surprises.
Can you come out the same day? ▼
Usually yes. Call before 11am and we'll almost always get a technician to you the same day. After that, next-day service is standard.
Is there a service fee just to come out? ▼
There's a $79 diagnostic fee for the visit, but we waive it completely if you go ahead with the repair. So if we fix it, the visit is free.
What brands do you repair? ▼
All major brands — Whirlpool, Samsung, LG, GE, Maytag, KitchenAid, Bosch, Frigidaire, Kenmore, Electrolux, Sub-Zero, Viking, and pretty much anything else. If you don't see your brand listed, just ask.
Do you offer a warranty? ▼
Yes — every repair comes with a 90-day workmanship warranty. If the same issue returns within 90 days, we come back and fix it at no charge.
Is it worth repairing an old appliance? ▼
Usually yes, if the repair costs less than half of replacement. Our techs will tell you honestly if a repair makes sense or if you're better off buying new. We don't push repairs that don't make financial sense.
